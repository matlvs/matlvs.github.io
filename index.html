Mathieu Le Verge-Serandour

Postdoctoral researcher · Biological Physics and Morphogenesis group (Prof. Karen Alim) · Technical University of Munich

I am a theoretical physicist working at the interface with biology. I study how living systems use flows, pressure and transport to shape themselves and to process information: from the adaptive tube networks of the slime mould Physarum polycephalum to the fluid-filled cavities of the early mammalian embryo.

My work combines physical modelling, numerical simulations and quantitative microscopy, and I collaborate closely with experimental groups.
